1. Getting Started & Presets

Mercer is built for Shopify Online Store 2.0 with full Sections Everywhere support, app blocks, and two distinct presets:

  • Mercer: High-contrast light editorial palette (#FFFFFF background, #08141E ink, #E86A17 accent) with rounded 22px Bento cards.
  • Mercer Noir: Dark architectural aesthetic (#0B0E11 background, #F5F5F4 ink, #D97706 accent) with tighter 12px corners.

To switch presets, open Online Store > Themes > Customize, open Theme settings, and select your preferred theme style.

Before editing code: Always duplicate your theme in Online Store > Themes (click the ... menu > Duplicate) before making any custom code edits.

2. Theme Settings

Setting Group What It Controls
Colors Define up to four reusable color schemes (background, gradient, text, primary button, outline button, hairline border) plus your global Brand accent color.
Typography Select your Heading font (default: Anton), Heading scale (85%–130%), Uppercase display headings toggle, and Body font (default: Inter).
Layout and shape Adjust Max page width (1200px–1680px), Grid gap (8px–32px), and Bento card corner radius (0px–32px).
Animations and badges Toggle the Fly-to-Cart animation, adjust its duration (800ms–2400ms), and enable or hide rotating studio stamp badges globally.
Cart drawer Enable the free shipping progress bar, set the threshold in your store currency (e.g., 250 for $250), and toggle the order note field.
Social media & Favicon Add links for Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, and X (displayed automatically in the footer) and upload your 32 x 32px .png favicon.

3. Header, Navigation & Footer

Announcement bar

Add up to 6 announcements with optional links. Toggle Enable scrolling marquee to turn announcements into a continuous horizontal ticker and adjust Marquee duration (seconds) to control speed.

Header & Mega Menu

Assign your Main menu (default: main-menu). Mercer supports up to 3-level nested menus: top-level items with sub-links automatically open a full-width editorial mega menu on desktop and collapsible accordions in the mobile drawer.

  • Mega menu feature blocks: Add a Mega menu feature block in the Header section and enter the exact Parent menu item title (e.g., Shop) to attach editorial promo imagery inside that dropdown.
  • Customer accounts: Mercer integrates Shopify's native <shopify-account> component in both desktop and mobile headers.

Footer

Configure up to 4 columns using Menu column and Brand note blocks. Enable the scrolling marquee (separate phrases with a pipe |), newsletter signup, Follow on Shop button, country/language selectors, store policies, and full-color payment icons.

4. Home Page Sections

  • Editorial hero: Supports separate Desktop image and Mobile image, optional auto-playing background video, scrim opacity slider, primary/secondary buttons, rotating 360-degree studio stamp, and scrolling marquee.
  • Brand manifesto split: High-contrast split card with Image position on desktop (Left or Right), Heading, and Highlighted sticker word (automatically wraps the matching word in a tilted sticker badge).
  • Featured collection: Displays a 3-piece asymmetric Bento showcase with category filter pills. Increasing Products to show above 3 automatically renders an aligned 4-column product grid underneath.
  • Neo-Bento seasons grid: 4-tile asymmetric seasonal showcase (Autumn, Spring with rotating stamp, accent-colored Summer editorial tile, and Winter) linked directly to collections.
  • Shop the look: Full-width campaign banner with split left/right sticker headlines (Left heading, Left sticker text, Right sticker text, Right heading) and up to 5 interactive Garment hotspot pins that open a slide-over outfit drawer.
  • Featured product & Custom Liquid: Available on all JSON templates, with full support for @app blocks and custom Liquid snippets.

5. Product Page & Metafields

The Product information section supports drag-and-drop blocks for Brand, Title, Price, Model fit notes, Variant picker, Buy buttons, Delivery and trust signals, Description, Collapsible rows, Custom Liquid, and App blocks.

Color swatches

Mercer supports Shopify's native Category Metafield color and image swatches automatically on options named Color, Colour, Fabric, or Shade. To display text pills instead of swatches, uncheck Show color swatches inside the Variant picker block.

Size guide & Model fit metafields

  1. In Shopify Admin, go to Settings > Metafields and metaobjects > Products.
  2. Create a single-line text metafield with key custom.model_fit to show per-product fit notes (e.g., Model is 6'1" wearing size L).
  3. Create a rich text or page reference metafield with key custom.size_guide for product-specific sizing charts, or assign a global fallback in the section's Default size guide page setting.

Native Shopify Commerce Features

  • Related & Complementary products: Install the free Shopify Search & Discovery app to curate complementary pairings displayed in the Complementary products section and related items in Related products.
  • Rich media & 3D/AR: Upload MP4 videos, YouTube/Vimeo links, or 3D GLB/USDZ models directly to product media. Mobile shoppers automatically see the View in your space AR button.
  • Local pickup, Unit pricing & Gift cards: Pickup availability, unit prices, Shop Pay Installments, dynamic checkout buttons, and gift card recipient fields work automatically when enabled in Shopify Admin.

6. Collections & Search Filtering

Mercer uses Shopify's native Storefront Filtering on both Collection and Search pages. To configure filters (Availability, Price, Size, Color, Vendor, Product type), install and open the free Shopify Search & Discovery app and select Filters.

On desktop collection pages, shoppers can switch between 2, 3, and 4 columns via the toolbar switcher, and their preference is remembered across page loads.

7. Cart Drawer & Animations

Mercer includes an editorial slide-out cart drawer with a live free shipping progress bar, quantity controls, selling plan labels, line-level and order-level discount displays, unit prices, order notes, and a Complete the Look collection upsell list.

To configure the upsell list, select the Cart drawer section in the Theme Editor and choose a collection. Up to 3 in-stock products are displayed for one-click adding.

8. Frequently Asked Questions

What image aspect ratios work best with Mercer?

For product cards and galleries, a 3:4 or 4:5 portrait ratio (at least 1200 x 1500px .jpg) delivers the sharpest look. Mercer automatically adapts to 16:9, 4:3, 3:2, and 1:1 images without stretching or cropping full product photos on product pages.

How do I customize or translate the marquee phrases?

Open the Editorial hero, Product information, or Footer section in the Theme Editor and edit the Marquee phrases field. Separate each phrase with a pipe character (|), or uncheck Show scrolling marquee to hide it.

Why is my Complementary products section hidden on a product page?

The Complementary products section stays hidden until products are assigned via the Shopify Search & Discovery app, preventing empty gaps on products without pairings.

Can I turn off the Fly-to-Cart animation?

Yes. Go to Theme settings > Animations and badges and uncheck Enable Fly-to-Cart animation. The animation also respects visitors' prefers-reduced-motion system settings automatically.
